<h2 style="margin: 60px 0px 10px;">Short Bio</h2>

I am currently a Postdoctoral Fellow at [King Abdullah University of Science and Technology (KAUST)](https://www.kaust.edu.sa/en/) supervised by [Prof. Xin Gao](https://www.kaust.edu.sa/en/study/faculty/xin-gao). Before joining KAUST in May 2026, I worked as an Algorithm Engineer at [Alibaba Group](https://www.alibabagroup.com/en-US/) from September 2024, where I focused on advertisement and recommendation systems. I received my PhD in Computer Science from the University of Liverpool in 2024, under the supervision of [Prof. Kaizhu Huang](https://sites.google.com/view/kaizhu-huang-homepage) and [Prof. Xinping Yi](https://sites.google.com/site/xinpingyi00/). My earlier research explored machine learning under real-world heterogeneity, including graph learning and online distribution shift adaptation ([a brief intro](https://jingweio.github.io/assets/files/selfIntro_JingweiGuo.pdf)). My current research interests center on AI for protein science and agentic workflows for protein design.

## Research Interest

- **Protein Design**
- **Graph Machine Learning** 
- **Transfer Learning**
- **Medical Image Analysis**
